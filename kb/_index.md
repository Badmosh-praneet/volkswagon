## About Volkswagen India

Volkswagen India Private Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of Volkswagen AG, Germany.
The company commenced operations in India in 2007.
Its state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is located in Chakan, Pune, Maharashtra.
Volkswagen India offers a lineup of German-engineered cars across SUV and sedan segments.

### Manufacturing

- **Plant Location:** Chakan, Pune, Maharashtra, India
- **Plant Type:** Fully integrated manufacturing plant
- **Platform:** MQB-A0-IN (India-specific MQB platform)
- **Capacity:** High-volume production for domestic & export

### Technology Highlights

- **TSI Engine:** Turbocharged Stratified Injection — efficient petrol engines
- **DSG Gearbox:** Direct Shift Gearbox — dual-clutch automatic transmission
- **MQB-A0-IN:** Modular Transverse Matrix platform designed for India
- **IQ.DRIVE:** Suite of driver assistance systems
- **ABS + ESC:** Standard safety across all models
- **6 Airbags:** Standard on all models
