​To celebrate this journey and the passion of its drivers, Volkswagen introduces the Virtus Anniversary Edition. ### **5-star GNCAP safety **


Volkswagen Virtus achieved a 5-star rating in Global NCAP's updated crash tests, becoming one of the first safest sedan models ever in India to achieve five stars for both adult and child occupants' protection. Equipped with 40 safety features and the best of German-engineering, this badge is a testament to Volkswagen ’s continued commitment to engineering for unmatched safety. At Volkswagen , safety has been at the core of our German DNA and this badge is a reaffirmation of the faith our customers, like you, have always had.

### **What is the Virtus mileage (fuel efficiency)? **


The Volkswagen Virtus mileage depends on the engine option. The Virtus engine lineup includes a 1.0L TSI and a 1.5L TSI, both built with advanced TSI technology. Check the brochure to know more about variant-specific mileage figures.
