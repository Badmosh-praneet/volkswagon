### **Clocking record speed **


At NATRAX, Asia's largest test track, VW India unleashed the 2025 Golf GTI in full hot‑hatch glory, setting a new national record for the highest speed achieved by a front‑wheel‑drive (FWD) car, clocking 254.6 km/h.


Sporting a 2.0L TSI engine tuned for 261 hp (370 Nm), paired with a 7‑speed DSG and electronic locking differential, the GTI blasted from 0–100 km/h in 5.9 s and hit a top speed of 267 km/h during two controlled hot laps, remaining rock‑solid even on the banked curves. Comprehensive media coverage highlighted its aggressive yet refined handling, tartan‑accented interior, and the familiar GTI design language with illuminated grille, 18″ wheels, and dual exhausts.
