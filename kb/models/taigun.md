### **Explore the new Taigun **


### **It has the sort of shape that improves your plans **


The new Taigun wears its sharper lines, sculpted bonnet, redesigned bumpers, strong dual shoulder lines, and striking alloy wheels with easy confidence. Add the GT red lettering on door, and there’s just enough attitude in the details to suggest this is not a car for cutting drives short. One look at it, and the long way home starts making sense again.


### **Take your time **


There’s no need to hurry a drive that feels this good. The new Taigun brings that ease to the road with 1.0L and 1.5L TSI engine options, and a new-generation 8-speed automatic transmission. So you stay with the road a little longer, let the song finish, and don’t mind if home can wait.


### **Get in. Go on. **


A refreshed dashboard, dual-tone premium interiors, leatherette seats with piping, contrast stitching, GT embroidery and Laser Red ambient lighting make the new Taigun feel like a very good reason not to head straight home.
