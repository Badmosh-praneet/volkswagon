## **Beyond SmootheR​ **


Beneath the striking exterior lies a powerhouse driven by a 2.0 TSI EVO engine, intelligent 4MOTION all-wheel drive, and a 7-speed DSG transmission.

It produces an impressive 204 PS (150 kW) of power and 320 Nm of torque, ensuring instant response to every command.​


## **Beyond BoldeR​ **


Own the road with a design that stands out. LED headlamps, an Illuminated light bands, and sporty ‘R’ themed details shape a sophisticated profile. The muscular front and rear shoulder lines add to the bold stance, while the rounder wheel arches and R 19” Coventry wheels infuse elegance into its sharp character.


## **Beyond SafeR​ **


Confidence on the road is engineered into every aspect of the drive. 21 Level 2 ADAS features, provides intelligent assistance and enhanced safety, including Lane Assist, Front Assist, and comprehensive protection with disc brakes and 9 airbags, ensuring peace of mind is part of the journey.​
