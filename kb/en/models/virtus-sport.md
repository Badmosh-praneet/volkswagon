**Virtus Anniversary Edition
**
Four years of being India’s No.1 Premium Sedan is more than a milestone. It celebrates the journey of the Virtus and the passion of those who continue to choose it. The Virtus GT Plus Sport stays true to what makes it special, with its 1.5L TSI EVO engine, 150 PS of power, 250 Nm of torque and a responsive 7-speed DSG transmission.​


​The Virtus Anniversary Edition gives the drive a distinctive new look. Finished in exclusive Avocado Pearl with a contrasting black roof and black alloy wheels, it enhances the sedan’s sporty and premium character. With Volkswagen Genuine accessories such as a 360° Camera System, Front Parking Sensors and Puddle Lamps, and limited to just 150 units, it brings even more desirability to every drive.

### 24-hours Endurance Drive


Volkswagen cars were put to the ultimate test at Asia's largest track, NATRAX, for 24 gruelling hours. The Virtus 1.5L TSI EVO and Virtus 1.0L TSI engines showcased the power and efficiency setting new records in the India Book of Records.


Indian Record for the maximum distance covered in 24 hours (overall): VW Virtus GT 1.5L, 4654.48 km*
