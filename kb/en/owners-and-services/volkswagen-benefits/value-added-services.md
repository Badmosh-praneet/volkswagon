# **Value Added Services **


A Volkswagen is built to perform and endure. Our Value-Added Services (VAS) are designed to make sure it stays that way. From UV paint protection and ceramic coatings to interior care and precision windshield treatments, each service is carried out by trained Volkswagen technicians using approved processes available at Volkswagen dealerships across India.
