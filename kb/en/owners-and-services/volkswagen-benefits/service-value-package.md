## **Frequently Asked Questions **


###


-


### What is a Service Value Package (SVP)?


SVP is a prepaid service package that covers the periodic maintenance of your Volkswagen vehicle as per the terms and conditions mentioned in the Owner’s Manual, based on the age and mileage of the vehicle.


-


### What services are covered under SVP?


Preventive maintenance services as specified in the Owner’s Manual and included in the specific SVP package list are covered.


-


### Who is eligible to buy an SVP?


Any customer owning a Volkswagen vehicle can purchase an SVP from any authorized dealership.


-


### Where can I avail services under the SVP?


You can avail services at any authorized Volkswagen service center across the country.


-


### How do I claim SVP services?


Book a service appointment at any authorized Volkswagen workshop.


-


### How long is an SVP valid?


SVP is valid for the number of services covered or until the contract end date, whichever occurs earlier.


-


### Is SVP transferable?


Yes, SVP is transferable to the new owner upon vehicle resale.


-


### Can I cancel or get a refund?


SVPs are non-refundable and non-cancellable once activated.


-


### What is not covered under SVP?


Accidental damages, wear-and-tear items, cosmetic repairs, insurance-related jobs, and warranty repairs.


-


### How do I purchase an SVP?


Visit an authorized Volkswagen dealership or book online.


-


### Do I need to pay for parts and labour?


No, parts and labour covered under SVP are included.
