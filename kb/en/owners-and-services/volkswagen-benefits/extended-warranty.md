### Experience Peace of mind with Volkswagen ’s Extended Warranty


Indulge in worry-free driving in your Volkswagen for years to come with our Extended Warranty, ensuring peace of mind throughout all your journeys. ### Highlights of up to +2 years Extended Warranty


-


Extended warranty for 4+1 year/ 1,25,000 km (covering 5 th year)

-


Extended warranty for 4+2 years/ 1,50,000 km (covering 5 th and 6 th year)


-


### Buying Period


-


Extended Warranty to be availed within 4 years of new vehicle warranty period

-


Flexibility to avail extended warranty at different intervals within 4 years of new vehicle warranty at attractive price in various time intervals
0-365 days (1st year of ownership)
366-730 days (2nd year of ownership)
731-1430 days ( 3rd and 4th year of ownership up to 1 month before end of warranty)
